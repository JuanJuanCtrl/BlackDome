# [SYNOPSIS]

<p>A dark, thrilling visual novel about a teenage boy who falls into an underground dome, and must find a way out.</p> 
<p>He does not remember anything from his past life, but as he gets to know the people and his surroundings...</p>
<p>...he questions if returning to the surface is really the best idea.</p>

<br><br>

# [PROGRESS]

<p>As of right now, the game is at a very early stage of development. The game will likely be finished at the end of the first school semester.</p>
<p>That's the game itself, but the story manuscript which I'm currently writing is in a good shape. I'm almost nearing the end of the first chapter's writing phase.</p>
<p>Which also is the equivalent of what I'll be doing for the whole game. Once the main storyline is finished, I will begin writing the game's second route branch.</p>
<p>It will take some time, but it's at a steady pace. I believe it will turn out great once finished.</p>
